About yourads

yourads is an AI-powered ads manager. It brings campaign management for Meta, TikTok and Google Ads together with an AI Studio for ad copy, image and video creatives, and WhatsApp lead handling, so small teams can plan, create and run advertising in one place.

Who is behind it

yourads brings together two companies: KubeAI LLC, which builds AI software, and Siembra Films, a production company that makes film, video and advertising content.

KubeAI LLC

KubeAI LLC builds swarm-agent software: instead of relying on one model for one task, it runs several specialized agents in parallel, each analyzing a different signal and producing its own confidence score, and combines their results into a final decision. It describes this as “the intelligence layer above the model,” with multi-agent orchestration, fallback handling, signal weighting and workflow composition.

Siembra Films

Siembra Films SAS is a production company based in Bogotá, Colombia, working on film, video, programs, advertising and television commercials.
