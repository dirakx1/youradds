Data Deletion

Last updated: November 18, 2026

You can stop youradds from accessing your Meta (Facebook and Instagram) data and ask us to delete the information we hold about you at any time.

1. Disconnect Meta right away

Removing youradds from your Facebook account immediately stops our access to your ad account, Pages and Instagram account:

  1. Open Facebook, then Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations.
  2. Find youradds and choose Remove.

You can also remove the saved Meta connection inside youradds, under Connections.

2. Ask us to delete your data

Send an email to contact@youradds.co with the subject “Data deletion request”, from the email address of your youradds account. Tell us if you want your whole account deleted or only your Meta connection and the data imported from Meta.

We will delete your saved platform credentials and access tokens, and the campaigns, metrics, creatives, leads and messages stored in your account, then confirm by email. Campaigns and ads that already exist in Meta are not changed by this; manage or delete them in Meta Ads Manager.

3. What we may keep

We may keep limited information where the law permits or requires it, for example billing records, and backups until they are rotated out. See our Privacy Policy for details.
