How to get your Meta Ads access token

You need two things to connect Meta in youradds: an access token and your ad account ID. This guide uses a “system user”, which gives a token that doesn't expire after a few hours. It takes about 10 minutes.

Before you start

  • A Facebook account that is an admin of your Business Manager (business.facebook.com).
  • An ad account and, if you will publish ads, a Facebook Page inside that Business Manager.

Step by step

  1. Create a Meta app. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps, click Create app, choose the Other use case and the Business type. Name it (for example “youradds”) and link it to your Business Manager. Then add the Marketing API product.
  2. Open business settings. Go to business.facebook.com/settings and pick your business.
  3. Create a system user. Under Users → System users click Add, enter a name (for example “youradds”) and choose the Admin role.
  4. Give it access to your ad account. With the user selected, click Assign assets → Ad accounts, tick your account and turn on Full control (manage campaigns). If you will publish ads, also assign your Page.
  5. Generate the token. Click Generate new token, pick your app and, if offered, the option that never expires. Tick the permissions ads_management, ads_read and business_management. Click Generate token.
  6. Copy the token. Copy the long text that appears (it starts with EAA…). Meta shows it only once: store it somewhere safe and never share it.
  7. Find your ad account ID. In Ads Manager the ID is in the account selector, or in the URL as act=1234567890. In youradds you can enter it with or without act_.
  8. Paste them into youradds. Open Connections, paste the token and the ID into the Meta card and click Save. Then click Test & import campaigns to check it works.

Common problems

  • “Credentials rejected”: check you copied the whole token with no spaces and that the system user has the ad account assigned.
  • No never-expires option: the token still works; if it expires, generate a new one and replace it in Connections.
  • Security: youradds stores your token encrypted. If you think it leaked, revoke it under System users.
