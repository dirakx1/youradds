# Managed Service Terms: DRAFT for legal review

**Status: draft, not published.** Written 2026-11-22 from how the product really behaves (Phase 3 of `docs/multiuser-meta-plan.md`). It is an addendum to the existing Terms of Service (`frontend/en/terms`, last updated October 31, 2026). Nothing here is legal advice; every clause marked **[LAWYER]** needs a decision or review. When approved, it becomes a page (`/en/managed-terms/` and `/es/managed-terms/`, like the other legal pages) and the request button should ask the client to accept it (see "Product follow-ups").

## Points to decide before publishing

1. **Fee model [OWNER].** Section 9 is a placeholder. Decide: flat monthly fee, percentage of ad spend, or both; billing currency (USD or COP); who invoices and how (card, bank transfer); taxes.
2. **Liability [LAWYER].** Section 12 reuses the Terms of Service cap (the greater of 12 months of fees or USD $100). Confirm it is acceptable when staff spend a client's advertising budget, and whether to exclude or cap losses from ad spend the client approved.
3. **Notice to end [LAWYER].** Clients can end at any time with one click. Section 10 gives youradds 30 days' notice (immediately for breach, legal risk or platform action). Confirm.
4. **Customers in Colombia [LAWYER].** Most clients are Colombian businesses. Confirm consumer and data-protection rules (Ley 1581 de 2012 and its regulations, the SIC's rules on international transfers of personal data), whether the governing law clause can stay "United States of America", and whether the Spanish version prevails for Spanish-speaking clients.
5. **Authority to act [LAWYER].** Section 4 has the client authorise youradds to act on their ad account. Confirm the wording is enough to count as a delegation under Meta's partner terms.
6. **Indemnity.** Section 12 has none. Decide whether to add one for the client's content and claims.
7. **Audit log retention [OWNER].** Section 11 says we keep the activity record "for [24] months" after the service ends. Choose the period. The product does not delete old records automatically yet; that would have to be built before promising it.
8b. **Emails depend on SES.** Sections 5 and 6 promise approval and summary emails. They only work once SES is configured and out of sandbox; do not publish before that.
8. **Meta's terms.** Review Meta's Business Tools / Partner terms for anything that constrains this model (for example rules on managing other businesses' ad accounts and on disclosing the arrangement).

## Product follow-ups once approved

- Show the terms (with a version and date) when a client presses "Ask youradds to manage my ads", require a tick to accept, and store who accepted which version and when.
- Add a link to the terms on the checklist card and in the management emails.
- Update the privacy policy to mention staff access to managed accounts and the activity record.
